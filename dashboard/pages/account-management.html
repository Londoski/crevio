Deactivate this account?

If you deactivate your account:

  • No one will see your account and content.
  • Information that isn't stored in your account, such as direct messages, may still be visible to clients (others).
  • Crevio will continue to keep your data so that you can recover it when you reactivate your account.
  • You can reactivate your account and recover all content anytime by using the same login details.

Delete account permanently?

Your account and content will be deleted permanently. You may cancel the deletion request by reactivating your account within 30 days.
